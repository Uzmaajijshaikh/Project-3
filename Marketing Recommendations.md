Marketing Recommendations – E-Commerce Customer Segmentation

1. Introduction
The objective of this analysis was to segment e-commerce customers using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis and K-Means clustering. The results help identify valuable customers, customers at risk of leaving, and opportunities to improve customer retention and revenue.

2. Key Findings
1. Total Customers: 4,338
2. Champions: 942 customers (21.7%)
3. At Risk: 661 customers (15.2%)
4. Lost Customers: 366 customers (8.4%)
5. Potential Loyalists: 1,551 customers (35.8%)
6. K-Means Clusters: 4 customer groups
The largest RFM segment is Potential Loyalists, while Champions represent an important group of high-value, engaged customers. At-Risk and Lost Customers offer opportunities for targeted re-engagement campaigns.

3. Marketing Recommendations
1. Champions: Offer VIP rewards, early access to new products, exclusive discounts, and referral incentives to retain high-value customers.
2. Loyal Customers: Introduce loyalty points, personalized product recommendations, and complementary-product offers to encourage repeat purchases.
3. Potential Loyalists: Send personalized recommendations and second-purchase incentives to convert promising customers into loyal buyers.
4. Recent Customers: Provide welcome offers, helpful follow-up messages, and product suggestions to encourage another purchase.
5. At-Risk Customers: Run targeted win-back email campaigns, offer relevant limited-time discounts, and remind customers of products they previously purchased.
6. Lost Customers: Test low-cost reactivation campaigns and avoid spending heavily on customers who do not respond.

4. K-Means Cluster Strategies
1. High-Value Loyal Customers (Cluster 0): Prioritize VIP benefits, retention offers, and referral programs. This cluster has the highest average monetary value and frequency.
2. Inactive / At-Risk Customers (Cluster 1): Use reactivation campaigns and personalized incentives. These customers have the highest average recency, meaning they have been inactive for longer.
3. Recent / Newer Customers (Cluster 2): Encourage second and third purchases through follow-up offers and product recommendations.
4. Regular Customers (Cluster 3): Increase purchase frequency through loyalty rewards, cross-selling, and product bundles.

5. Success Measures
Track repeat purchase rate, customer retention, campaign conversion rate, revenue per customer, and reactivation rate to evaluate campaign effectiveness.

6. Conclusion
Combining RFM analysis with K-Means clustering enables more targeted marketing strategies. Prioritizing valuable customers, nurturing potential loyalists, and re-engaging inactive customers can help improve customer retention and support sustainable revenue growth.
